 The program will randomly select one of three options: 'Rock', 'Paper', or 'Scissors'. This is analogous to the computer generating the random number (1-100) in the guessing game.

The program will prompt the player to enter their choice (R, P, or S).

The program will compare the player's choice to the computer's choice and apply the rules:

Rock crushes Scissors.

Scissors cuts Paper.

Paper covers Rock.

If choices are the same, it's a tie.

print the computer's choice and the final outcome (Win, Lose, or Tie).

Like the guessing game continues until a winner emerges, wrap game in a loop so the player can play multiple rounds until they decide to quit.
